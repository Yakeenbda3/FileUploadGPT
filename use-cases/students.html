Sample Study Prompts

Copy these prompts after uploading your study materials.

📚 Chapter Summary
"Summarize this chapter highlighting the key concepts, important definitions, and main arguments. Create bullet points I can use for studying."
❓ Practice Quiz
"Based on this material, create 20 practice questions that might appear on an exam. Include a mix of multiple choice, short answer, and essay questions."
💡 Concept Explanation
"Explain the concept of [topic] from this text in simpler terms. Use analogies and real-world examples to help me understand it better."
📝 Study Guide
"Create a detailed study guide from these lecture notes. Organize by topic, highlight what I need to memorize vs. understand, and note any potential exam questions."
🔗 Connect Concepts
"How does [concept A] from this chapter relate to [concept B]? Help me understand the connections between these ideas."

A Note on Academic Integrity

FileUploadGPT is designed to help you learn and understand material better, not to do your work for you. Always follow your school's academic integrity policies. Use this tool to enhance your understanding, create study materials, and prepare for exams — not to submit AI-generated work as your own.

When in doubt, ask your instructor about appropriate AI use in their course.
