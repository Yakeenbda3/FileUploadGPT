Sample Prompts for Researchers

Copy these prompts after uploading your research documents.

📄 Literature Summary
"Summarize the key findings, methodology, and limitations of this research paper. Identify the main contribution to the field."
🔍 Research Gap Analysis
"Based on this paper, what research gaps or unanswered questions does the author identify? What future research directions do they suggest?"
📊 Methodology Evaluation
"Evaluate the research methodology used in this study. What are its strengths and potential weaknesses? Are there any threats to validity?"
✍️ Writing Feedback
"Review this draft section for academic writing quality. Suggest improvements for clarity, flow, and argumentation strength."
📈 Statistical Analysis Help
"Analyze this dataset. What patterns do you notice? What statistical tests would be appropriate to answer [specific research question]?"
