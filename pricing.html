Why Is FileUploadGPT Free?

We built FileUploadGPT because we were frustrated with ChatGPT's file size limits. We needed a solution, so we built one.

Rather than keep it to ourselves, we decided to share it with the community. We believe useful tools should be accessible to everyone, not locked behind paywalls.

Our philosophy is simple:

  • Build something useful
  • Keep it simple
  • Give it away for free
  • Maintain it with care

FileUploadGPT has no premium tier, no "pro" version, and no plans to add one. The free version is the full version.

Common Questions

Will FileUploadGPT always be free?

Yes! We have no plans to change our pricing model. FileUploadGPT will remain free for all users, forever.

How do you sustain the project?

FileUploadGPT is a passion project. Since all processing happens locally in your browser, there are no server costs. We maintain it because we use it ourselves and want it to exist.

Are there any usage limits?

No. You can upload as many files as you want, as often as you want. There are no daily, weekly, or monthly limits.

Do I need a ChatGPT Plus subscription?

No. FileUploadGPT works with both the free version of ChatGPT and ChatGPT Plus. Your ChatGPT subscription is separate from FileUploadGPT.
