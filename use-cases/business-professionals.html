Sample Prompts for Business

Copy these prompts after uploading your business documents.

📊 Executive Summary
"Create a one-page executive summary of this report. Include key findings, metrics, recommendations, and any risks or concerns mentioned."
📋 Contract Review
"Analyze this contract and identify: key obligations for each party, termination clauses, liability limitations, and any unusual or concerning terms."
🎯 Meeting Action Items
"Extract all action items from this meeting transcript. For each, identify: the task, who is responsible, any mentioned deadline, and context."
📈 Financial Analysis
"Analyze this financial data. Calculate key ratios, identify trends, highlight any anomalies, and summarize the overall financial health."
🔍 Competitive Intel
"Based on this competitor report, summarize their strategy, strengths, weaknesses, and potential threats to our business."
