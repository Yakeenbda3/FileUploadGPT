Frequently Asked Questions

Is FileUploadGPT really free?

Yes! FileUploadGPT is completely free to use. There are no subscriptions, hidden fees, or account requirements. We believe in making tools accessible to everyone.

Does FileUploadGPT work with ChatGPT Plus and the free version?

Yes, FileUploadGPT works seamlessly with both ChatGPT Plus and the free version of ChatGPT. No matter which plan you're on, you can upload large files.

Is my data safe with FileUploadGPT?

Absolutely. FileUploadGPT processes your files locally in your browser. We never upload, store, or access your file contents on any external server. Your data stays on your device until you choose to send it to ChatGPT.

What file types does FileUploadGPT support?

FileUploadGPT supports a wide range of file types including PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD (Markdown), HTML, CSV, JSON, and most common text-based file formats. We're constantly adding support for more formats.

How do I install the extension?

Simply click the "Install Free Extension" button on this page, which takes you directly to the Chrome Web Store. Click "Add to Chrome" and the extension will be installed in seconds. No account creation or configuration required.
